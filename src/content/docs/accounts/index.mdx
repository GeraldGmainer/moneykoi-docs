## How sync behaves

| | What the user gets |
|---|---|
| **Reads** | from the phone's own database — never wait on the network |
| **Writes** | on screen at once; pushed in the background, in order, one at a time |
| **Other devices' changes** | a realtime event starts a pull; app resume and reconnect pull too |
| **Conflicts** | last write wins, no field merging; a pull never overwrites an unpushed edit |
| **Offline** | everything works; a pending dot shows until pushed; 3 failures pause an item (red dot) |
| **First sync** | paged, oldest first; resumes after a failure (30 s → 60 s → 2 min → 5 min) or a kill |
| **A local book** | writes stay on the phone; nothing of the book's data is sent |

## The phone's states

The phone is a cache of books. Who is signed in decides which are visible; nothing is deleted by a logout.

| State | Account | Own book | Shared Cloud books | Features in own book | Leaves via |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Local Free** | – | on the phone | – | Free | *Delete all data*, uninstall |
| **Local Pro** | – | on the phone | – | Pro (store receipt) | as above; Pro stays with the store account |
| **Free account** | ✓ | on the phone, local | sync, every feature | Free, or Pro if bought | *Log out*, *Delete account* |
| **Paused** | ✓ | on the phone; the server keeps the copy of the pause | sync, every feature | Free, or Pro if owned | as above |
| **Cloud** | ✓ | syncs (up to 3 owned books) | sync, every feature | Cloud | as above |
| **Signed out** | (kept) | hidden on the phone | hidden | — | sign in again, or uninstall |

Free and Pro open **one own book at a time**; more own books are *Locked until Cloud*. Before **Jan 17 00:00 Europe/Vienna** every new account is an early adopter with Cloud for life, so free accounts first appear on Jan 17.

## Ways from a local book into an account

| Door | Where | What happens to the phone's book |
|---|---|---|
| **Back up & sync** (the claim) | Settings, Profile, the drawer's *Get Free Cloud* | with Cloud: uploaded; without: moved under the account, kept on the phone |
| **Join \{book\}** | an invite on a phone with a book and no account | never uploaded unless the account has Cloud; the shared book syncs |
| **Sign in / Sign back in** | Profile | not a claim — the book is set aside, closed after the logout until *Continue without an account* |

## Words

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **account** | a MoneyKoi sign-in (Google or email): how you use Cloud — your own, or a book shared with you |
| **book** | a household's data; each lives on the phone under its own id |
| **local book** | the one book of a device without an account |
| **Cloud book** | a book whose **owner** has Cloud; everyone in it gets every feature, only inside it |
| **free account** | an account with no plan of its own; its own book lives on the phone |
| **paused** | a former subscriber: sync stopped, the phone keeps working, the server keeps the copy of the pause |
| **claim** | moving a local book into an account (uploading it with Cloud) |
| **signed-out books** | an account's books kept on the phone after a logout, hidden until it signs in again |
