## What leaves the phone

| What | When | Where to | The user's control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Book data | only a book that syncs (Cloud) | MoneyKoi's Supabase (EU) | Cloud on / off |
| Receipt photos | only for a book that syncs | the book's storage | — |
| Live prices request | a book without an account, Pro, investments | MoneyKoi's Supabase, the whole list, anonymous | Settings → Privacy & Legal → *Live prices* |
| Crash reports | on by default | Sentry, EU region, scrubbed | opt-out |
| Usage statistics | only after a yes | MoneyKoi's Supabase, 400 days | Privacy & Legal → *Usage statistics* |
| Purchases | when buying or restoring | the store and RevenueCat | — |
| Invite emails | when inviting | MoneyKoi's mail function | — |
| Changelog | when opened online | MoneyKoi's Supabase, anonymous | — |
| Receipt text | never — read on the phone | — | — |

## The four features

| Feature | In one line | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **App lock** | PIN and/or biometrics over the whole app, from the first frame | Settings → App lock |
| **Hide amounts** | *Off* · *Tap to reveal* · *Always* — a blur, per device | Settings → App preferences |
| **Usage statistics** | opt-in, first-party, no SDK, erased on withdrawal | Privacy & Legal (app), Settings → Privacy & security (web) |
| **Use on computer** | the phone approves a browser by QR; the computer gets its own session | drawer (Cloud) |
