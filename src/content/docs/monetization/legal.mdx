<Aside type="caution">Not legal advice — a lawyer reads this page before the paywall ships (task T-MON-05 on [Open work](/monetization/open/)).</Aside>

1. **Launch price wording.** An announced price *reduction* must show the lowest price charged in the 30 days before it (EU Price Indication Directive 98/6/EC Art. 6a, via the Omnibus Directive 2019/2161; national rules in DE and AT). €69.99 was never charged, so **never** write "€20 off", "was €69.99" or a strikethrough. Write **"Launch price €49.99 until [date], then €69.99"**. A later sale from €69.99 is a real reduction once €69.99 has been charged.
2. **No false urgency.** Countdowns or scarcity counters that do not match reality are unfair commercial practices (UCPD Annex I). A real end date, stated plainly, is fine. No "only 200 left".
3. **Define "lifetime" in the Terms.** Lifetime Cloud promises a server — say what it means ("for as long as MoneyKoi Cloud is operated") and what happens if it ends. The early adopters' "for life" needs the same.
4. **App Review on the purchase screen:** subscription name, length and price per period, what a trial costs afterwards and when billing starts, Terms and Privacy links, *Restore purchases* without buying.
5. **The privacy policy must name:** RevenueCat (anonymous app user id, store receipt, purchase events, country, device metadata), Sentry (opt-out), the anonymous price fetch, the opt-in usage statistics.
6. **Sentry stays opt-out — Gerald's risk call (2026-10-14).** No regulator exempts crash reporting from device-access consent (ePrivacy Art. 5(3); AT TKG 2021 §165(3), DE TDDDG §25). Done to lower the risk: EU region, `sendDefaultPii` off, no Session Replay, screenshots or view hierarchy, user content scrubbed. Still open: a signed DPA with Sentry, the privacy policy naming it, the lawyer's view.
7. **Data portability.** The whole-book export stays free. GDPR Art. 20 applies to data on the server; locking the only way out behind a payment invites trouble.
8. **Selling outside the stores** (a web checkout) would make cancellation your job: Germany's cancellation button (§312k BGB) and the EU withdrawal flow. Inside the App Store and Google Play the stores handle it.
9. **Price-data attribution.** "Source: ECB statistics." is shown; Coinranking needs its attribution once it serves.
10. **Delete account vs. store billing.** The delete screen says a store subscription keeps billing until cancelled in the store, with *Manage subscription* — the lawyer reads that line too.
