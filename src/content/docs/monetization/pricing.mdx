## Launch price

- Cloud lifetime €49.99 and the upgrade €24.99 for **90 days** after the paywall ships.
- The paywall shows *Launch price until \{date\}* under the plan and *then \{regular\}* under the price — never a strikethrough or "was". See [Legal](/monetization/legal/).
- The end date is not set yet (D4); *then \{regular\}* stays off until the regular price has a source (D8). See [Open work](/monetization/open/).

## Early adopters

- **Cloud for life** for every **account** created before the paywall cutover (Jan 17).
- Account creation, not install date: the server cannot see local installs, so the launch copy says "create an account before Jan 17".
- Mechanism: a server backfill with a literal cutover timestamp (`20261007120000_early_adopter_grant.sql`), open-ended, its own `source`.
- "For life" must be defined in the Terms.

## Why lifetime at €69.99 is not a giveaway

Finance-app subscribers stay about 10.5 months on average, so a typical yearly subscriber renews once or twice (€26–35 net). A lifetime sale nets €49.56. A higher price would lose the subscription-averse buyers lifetime exists for.

## Target

€2,000 / month net ≈ 40 Pro sales per month + 700 active yearly subscribers + 6 lifetime sales per month.

After the launch, watch three thresholds in RevenueCat and the store consoles: download-to-paid below 1.5 %, annual → lifetime attach, Y1 retention.
