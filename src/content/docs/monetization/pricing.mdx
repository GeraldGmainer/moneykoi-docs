## Why lifetime €69.99 is fair

- finance subscribers stay ~10.5 months → 1–2 renewals (€26–35 net)
- lifetime nets €49.56
- higher → loses subscription-averse buyers

## Target

- €2,000 / month net ≈ 40 Pro + 700 yearly + 6 lifetime a month
- watch: download-to-paid < 1.5 % · annual → lifetime · Y1 retention
