## Ruled out

| Idea | Why |
|---|---|
| analytics SDK | costs the privacy sentence · X7 |
| a rival named or priced | store rules + UWG · D-PLAT-20 |
| a price in a store text | the store renders prices |
| *100 % on-device* · *100 % offline* | Sentry, OS backup, Cloud |
| paid ASO tool | the consoles show real terms |
| Google App Campaigns · Meta · TikTok · Reddit ads | €50 never leaves the learning phase |
| bought installs, reviews, "ASO boosts" | store policy · UWG Z 23c |
| *Cloud for life* again | time-limited prices instead |
| pages for untested banks or chains | claims what nobody checked |
| unedited AI pages | ranking risk |

## Outreach rules

- **no** reward for a rating · Play policy · UWG Z 23c
- free Pro to test · **never** tied to a review
- coverage for free Pro → labeled *Anzeige*
- a claim about a rival → dated, *Stand Nov 2026*
- synthetic voice → AI label on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube
- every comment, pitch, review reply **by hand**
- scheduling own posts may be automated
